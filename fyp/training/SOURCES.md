# Dataset sources and quality for the Colab experiment

The private-pool audit and grouped planner were rerun on 20 November 2026. The pool contained 95 registered front-display crops with zero independently approved annotations and zero verified device/session splits. Ten images are reserved for practice. No exact or near duplicates were flagged within this pool by the existing hash screen. Its 28 images with a short edge below 640 may lose thin-crack detail when resized. There were zero eligible new split assignments. No supervised smartphone training set or held-out research benchmark was produced.

### Private workspace inventory

The fresh audit found 95 decoded, attributable front-display crops, 10 reserved for practice and 85 waiting on device/session provenance. The original study supplied published visual markings. We matched, decoded and staged the 85 non-practice author overlays from the pinned release alongside the original crops. The normalized references have matching pixel dimensions and recorded source and normalized SHA-256 hashes. Reviewers can open them on demand in **Annotate**. Each target box or clean decision still requires an independent human approval. There are no class-labelled boxes in these references. Researchers should check the unmarked crop at full resolution before classifying the trace. The pool has 28 images below a 640 px short edge, including 25 non-practice crops. One crop exceeds mean grayscale intensity 215. These are review flags. The checkerboard test pattern limits automatic inference about sharpness, glare or whether a damage mark can be seen.

The exact/near-duplicate screen flagged zero pairs in the registered pool. It does not establish physical-device identity. The audit found zero train, validation or test images, zero reviewed clean images and zero approved instances of either target class. The split planner and model-proposal planner produced zero eligible items. The export gate remained closed. No new result or checkpoint was produced.

### Minimal handoff

1. From your university connection or normal browser try an [official SSGD Tsinghua link](https://cloud.tsinghua.edu.cn/f/720250d21e1b4887abf7/?dl=1) or [the alternate author link](https://cloud.tsinghua.edu.cn/f/0a03abd18c0f4038a9c4/?dl=1). The [author-listed Baidu link](https://pan.baidu.com/s/1Il2t5B0ns4SO8_HwTsmtKw?pwd=sted) is another option. If none works, request a fresh archive through the corresponding author in the paper or the official GitHub maintainer. Ask for the image and COCO label archive, physical capture/grouping metadata if available, and confirmation that academic processing on your private Drive/Colab is permitted. Supply the archive and license confirmation privately.
2. For a Roboflow alternative, supply **one** exact version URL, its license and primary source/permission evidence, then download the corresponding COCO or YOLO detection export through your own account. Include the dataset source/version notes and any device/session mappings. A code or project-level license alone cannot confirm upstream third-party image rights. For SSGD import, map exact labels `crack=generic_crack` and `scratch=scratch` when the annotation semantics support it. Other categories stay unmapped pending inspection. The importer preserves the source boxes as references for human review. [Importer command and options](../BENCHMARK_HANDOFF.md#accepting-a-new-authorized-dataset)
3. Use known device/session grouping before investing in labels. If unavailable for legacy images, collect consented photos with a private device/session register and reserve independent validation/test groups at capture. Each usable photo needs one exhaustive annotation or verified clean decision, then one independent approval. Test coverage requires at least 30 known devices, 30 instances per target class and 15 devices each with crack, scratch and reviewed clean coverage. Groups may satisfy multiple coverage targets. Training and validation each need both classes and reviewed clean examples. Model proposals can assist only the confirmed training partition.
