# ReValue AI FYP1 technical handoff

**Progress entry, 19 November 2026.** Audited the Python HTTP backend, active HTML app, catalogue, exterior model, grading and valuation rules, Gemini integration and evaluation script on `fyp1-development`. The bundle contains the YOLO11n weights and a historical evaluation summary, a normalized phone catalogue, Malaysia valuation calibration data and unlabelled prototype photos. No independent labelled detection split or segmentation masks were available in the checkout or supplied workspace files.

For a presentation, explain that the detector supplies **candidate boxes**, the evidence step decides which candidates can affect a **rule-based exterior grade**, and the valuation step estimates a **conditional price range**, based on model, storage, grade and available market evidence. A model confidence score does not quantify crack severity. An empty prediction list cannot demonstrate that a phone is undamaged. The grade-A rule is provisional in that case and reflects the supplied images and current detector.

Keep the historical results separate from future experiments. Do not compare historical precision and recall directly to the new evaluator's fixed-threshold, micro-averaged metrics unless the original evaluation conditions and averaging convention can be recovered. Neither the software fixtures nor simulated provider responses belong in the research results.
